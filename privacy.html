Fieldscape · Privacy · 27 September 2026

Your location stays with you

Fieldscape plays the field recordings placed around you as you walk. This page says, plainly, what that takes and where anything goes. It covers the website (keremaltaylar.github.io/Fieldscape) and the Fieldscape apps for iPhone and Android.

Your location

When you let Fieldscape use your location, it is used on your device only: to work out which recordings are near you and how loud each should be. It is not sent to Fieldscape or to anyone else, not stored on a server, and not shared.

To play a recording, your device downloads it. The server that holds the recordings sees that a recording was fetched, as any web server does, which says roughly which part of which park you are listening to. Fieldscape keeps no record of it beyond the storage provider's ordinary logs.

On a phone the walk can keep playing with the screen locked; your location is then still used on the device only. iPhone shows a blue location indicator while it does, and Android shows a notification you can pause the walk from.

No accounts for listeners, no tracking

Listening needs no account. Fieldscape uses no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies. Recordings you have heard are kept in your browser or app so they play offline next time; that stays on your device and you can clear it at any time (your browser's site data, or by removing the app).

Setters

People who place and publish recordings (setters) sign in with an email address. It is used only to sign you in and to record who published what; it is stored by the sign-in provider and never shown publicly.

What a setter publishes — recordings, photos, names, notes and where they are — is public, for anyone walking to hear. Photos are re-encoded before upload, so the location a phone camera writes into a photo is not kept. A point marked sensitive (a nest, a den, a rare species) is published at a fuzzed position, not its true one.

Who handles data

  • Supabase stores the published archive (recordings, photos, points and routes) and setters' sign-in.
  • Map imagery and maps come from Esri, OpenStreetMap and OpenTopoMap: your device asks them for the map tiles of the area on screen.
  • On the website, Google Fonts serves the typefaces and unpkg serves the map and archive libraries.

Each receives what any website or app request carries (such as your IP address) and handles it under its own privacy terms. Fieldscape sends none of them your location.

Questions, or to have something removed

Write to CONTACT_EMAIL. A setter's recordings and photos can be removed on request, and a setter account deleted.
