# Setter S2 — knobs, and the route patch panel

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** The route patch panel in the new look: every slider a knob (C-12), one full screen on a computer (C-8), tabs on a phone (M-2) — Chords · Voices · Effects · Morphs · Rhythm.

**Architecture:** `web/knobs.js` enhances any `input[type=range]` inside `#pp-body` / `#rp-body`: a knob drawn over it that sets the input's value and fires its `input` event, so the page's own handlers (pset, commitPatch, the readout) do everything; a MutationObserver re-enhances after every re-render. `web/patch.js` gives `#patchpanel` its phone tabs by tagging each section from its heading. `web/listener.css` restyles the panel.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-27-setter-redesign-design.md` (design 5; stage S2).

## Global Constraints

- The knob changes nothing but the input's value and fires `input` (and `change` on release); no patch logic moves.
- Knob: drag up/down (full range in ~200 px), fine drag with Shift or a long-press on touch (~1000 px), double-click / double-tap resets to `data-def`, arrow keys a step, Page keys ten, Home/End the ends; role=slider with aria-valuemin/max/now/valuetext and the row's label.
- Computer: the whole panel fits 1440×900 with no scroll inside or out. Phone: one tab at a time, no page scroll.

## Review Focus

1. A select that re-renders the panel mid-drag (instrument change) must not leave a dead knob: the observer re-enhances.
2. Tempo's `change` re-renders the panel: the knob fires `change` only on release, not on every move.
3. A knob on a range with step 1 (sectors) moves in whole steps.
4. Resizing across 860 px with the panel open: tabs appear/disappear, nothing lost.

## Tasks

1. **Knobs** — `web/knobs.js`, loaded after listener.js; tests in `core/tests/web_patch.mjs`: every range has a knob; a mouse drag up on Tempo raises the tempo (route patch) and the readout; ArrowUp adds a step; double-click resets to the default; touch drag works on the phone.
2. **The panel's look and fit** — `web/listener.css`: bar (title, Reset, Close), sections as knob cells, selects and toggles as rows, the progression and morphs; test: fits 1440×900 (panel scrollHeight ≤ clientHeight, page 0).
3. **Phone tabs** — `web/patch.js`: tab bar, sections tagged by heading, one tab shown; test: 5 tabs, Morphs tab shows the morph rows and hides Transport; each tab fits 390×844.
4. **Measure, look** — screenshots both sizes, contrast, all suites.
