How does a voice lane show where its settings come from?

The timeline runs along the route: 16 chords, left to right. Zones and sections cross the route somewhere along it, so they can show as bands on the same timeline. Precedence: zone > chord > section > route.

You see where along the walk each zone and section acts; a cell tinted by the band that changed it, outlined when a chord did. Click a band to edit that zone/section; click a cell to edit that chord.

My recommendation is C, with B's layered view available on demand (a "show layers" toggle per voice) for when you need to see exactly what is hiding under a zone.
