C · Map above, timeline docked below

The map keeps the top half with the route, zones and sections; the chord timeline and voice lanes dock underneath, with an inspector at the right. Selecting a chord cell lights its stretch of the route; selecting a zone on the map lights where it acts in the lanes. The dock can grow to full height for the bigger editors.

My recommendation is C: you edit in time (chords) and in space (zones, sections) at once, and both stay visible and linked.
